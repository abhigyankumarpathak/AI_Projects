How would you like to explore?

RAGScope can run a real AI model inside your browser, or show you a recorded demo. Nothing is downloaded until you choose.

Run the AI on my device

Checking whether your browser can run AI models…

  • Download: about 1.8 GB on first use (a 34 MB search model plus a 1.8 GB language model). Use Wi-Fi if you're on a data plan.
  • Where from: model files come from huggingface.co and raw.githubusercontent.com. The site's own code is served from this website, not from third parties.
  • Privacy: your questions never leave your device. There's no server, no account, no cookies and no tracking.
  • Safety: the models run in your browser's sandbox, like any web page. They can't read your files or reach anything outside this tab.
  • Storage: files are saved in your browser's cache so later visits load quickly. You can delete them at any time with Delete downloaded models at the top of the page.
  • Your device: needs about 2.5 GB of free memory. Your laptop may warm up and use more battery while the AI answers.

Watch the demo instead

  • No download. All four steps replay with real data recorded earlier from the same models.
  • You can pick from 5 example questions, but you can't type your own.
  • You can switch to the live AI later.

    Search the knowledge base

    Every passage of the AI's notes was embedded ahead of time. Here they are as dots, squashed from 384 dimensions to 2 so they fit on a screen. Your question is the star. The AI scores every passage by cosine similarity and keeps the closest few, connected by lines. The flat map distorts distances, so the scores on the right are what actually count.
